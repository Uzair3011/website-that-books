Common pricing questions

A little more clarity
on what you’re paying for.

What does the monthly plan cover?

The $399 monthly plan covers the managed system, website hosting, monitoring, support, 200 AI voice minutes, and 500 SMS segments. Allowances reset monthly and do not roll over. Additional usage, premium integrations, advertising, and major new features require an approved quote. We set usage limits and agree how inquiries are handled if a limit is reached.

How do the launch offer and guarantee work?

The launch setup price is $2,490 for scopes agreed and setup deposits paid by October 31, 2026, 11:59 p.m. America/Los_Angeles. After that, the bundle setup price is $3,490. Monthly management remains $399. The delivery guarantee applies to the agreed launch checklist, with a 14-day opportunity to fix failures. Read all offer and guarantee terms.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

Ongoing management is month-to-month after launch. You can cancel future service with 30 days’ notice. You own your domain, supplied content, and paid-for custom website work; third-party software remains subject to its own licensing.
