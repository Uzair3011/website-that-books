How does Website That Books work with my business?

We configure it around your service, booking rules, common questions, and customer journey. Your visitors get a better route to booking, while your team gets clearer context.

Can it handle both calls and customers from my website?

Yes. Website That Books can handle calls, website chat, booking-form enquiries, and the follow-up that comes after each interaction.

What happens when a customer needs a person?

Your team can be notified, receive the conversation context, and take over exactly when human attention is needed.

Can it work with my existing calendar?

It is designed to work with common calendar and business systems. We confirm the best setup for your current workflow during onboarding.

Can I see what is happening with my customers?

Yes. Customer details, conversation history, booking status, and next actions are kept in one clear view.
