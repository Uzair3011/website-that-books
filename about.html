Why med spas, specifically

One niche.
Built deliberately, not by accident.

We chose med spas because the fit is unusually strong — high-value visits, real repeat relationships, and a very real cost to a missed call.

Visits that are worth protecting.

AmSpa’s 2024 survey reports $527 average spend per visit and 73% repeat patients — a first inquiry is rarely a one-time event.

An owner who feels every gap.

Most med spas are independently run, single-location businesses — a missed call is felt directly, not absorbed by a large team.

Booking that lags behind demand.

Zenoti’s 2025 benchmarks put the average med spa’s online booking rate at just 11%. There’s real room to close that gap.

Where we draw the line

AI helps your front desk.
It doesn’t replace your judgment.

AI supports approved administrative questions, intake, and scheduling. It does not diagnose, determine treatment suitability, or replace clinical judgment — that stays with your team, always. We don’t promise a specific number of leads, bookings, or revenue: your results depend on your traffic, demand, offer, and follow-through. We say so plainly, in the terms and on every page where it matters.
