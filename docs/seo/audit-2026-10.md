## 3. Site-wide issues

### Critical

**C1. Client results presented as real, with nothing in the repo to support them, on the pages most likely to be ranked.** The five sales pages state these as outcomes for clients, in the past or present tense, under headings such as "Don't take our word for it. Look at the numbers." and "The Proof is in the Pipeline."

| Page | Claim as published |
|---|---|
| `/web-design-middlesbrough` | H1: "…Getting 3x More Local Leads Without Spending a Single Penny More on Ads." "Local Electrician": 2 leads a month became 18 "last month". "Local Joinery": "312% increase in inbound calls within 30 days of launch." "70% of your traffic is on a phone." |
| `/local-seo-middlesbrough` | "Local Plumber": "Page 3 of Google to the #1 Map Pack spot in Middlesbrough for 'Emergency Plumber'", "45 inbound calls generated directly from Google Maps last month." |
| `/landing-page-design` | "Google Ads Client": cost per lead £85 down to £12, "4x the leads for the exact same ad spend." Subheading "We Buy Clients For Pennies On The Pound." |
| `/ai-automation-middlesbrough` | "Real Lead Captured… books the appointment at 2:15 AM… £250 in revenue." |
| `/crm-booking-automation` | "40% of quotes sent were never followed up on", then "Automated SMS follow-ups increased quote-acceptance rate by 65%." |

The repo's own evidence points the other way:

- `src/service-visuals.js` (lines 1-4) says these visuals "are illustrative mock-ups, not client data" and that the copy beside each one carries the disclosure. The rendered pages carry no such disclosure: a search of the rendered text for "illustrative", "example" and "mock-up" finds nothing on any of the five pages.
- `/work` says Veltra Media is "a young studio", that its only real project is its own med-spa build, and that the other three are "designs for fictional businesses". `src/projects.js` and `docs/validation.md` (line 87) agree. `src/reviews.js` is empty.
- `/about` says "no invented results" in its meta description, and `/terms` says previews "are illustrations, not client results". The sales pages contradict both.
- `docs/seo/cursor-seo-prompts.md` already flags "312% more calls", "2 to 18 leads a month" and "£3,800 of value" as unverifiable.

This breaks hard rule 1 (no case-study figures). It also carries legal risk: UK consumer-protection law and the CAP/ASA codes treat fabricated testimonials and unsubstantiated performance claims as misleading. It undermines trust signals for search and for AI answer engines too, because the same site says elsewhere that results are not invented. Recommended action: remove the figures, or label each case plainly in the visible copy as an illustrative scenario, and settle the evidence question with the owner (owner question 1 below).

**C2. Ranking promises and "work for free" guarantees that contradict the rest of the site and hard rule 5.**

- `/local-seo-middlesbrough`: "The 'Top 3' Promise: We work for free until calls increase", H1 "If You Aren't in the Top 3 on Google Maps…", and "We hijack the Google Map Pack". `/web-design-middlesbrough` bonus text says "so you rank faster".
- Four other sales pages carry unconditional guarantees ("Live in 14 days, or you don't pay", "Beat your homepage, or we work for free", "Never miss a lead… or we work for free", "10+ hours a week of admin work saved, or we work for free").
- Against that, `/`, `/web-design-teesside`, `/google-business-profile`, `/about` and `/terms` say "No honest company can guarantee a ranking." Both positions cannot be true, and `/terms` does not appear to set out the terms of these guarantees.

**C3. Money-page titles and H1s do not match search intent.** The H1 is the strongest on-page signal after the title, and 13 of 18 non-legal pages have an H1 without the page's likely keyword.

- `/web-design-middlesbrough`: the page meant to rank for "web design Middlesbrough" has an H1 containing neither "web design" nor "Middlesbrough" ("How Teesside Service Businesses Are Getting 3x More Local Leads…", 109 characters).
- `/local-seo-middlesbrough`: the H1 never says "local SEO" or a place name.
- `/landing-page-design`: the H1 never says "landing page" (it says "1-Page System").
- `/crm-booking-automation`: the H1 has neither "CRM" nor "booking automation".
- `/ai-automation-middlesbrough`: the slug says "AI automation", the title and H1 say "AI receptionist", and the H1 has no place name.
- `/`: the title is 80 characters (limit 65), so Google will truncate or rewrite it.
- Beyond rankings, a searcher who typed "web design Middlesbrough" lands on a sales headline rather than an answer.

**C4. No pages for four of the six areas the business says it serves.** `SITE.areas` lists Middlesbrough, Stockton-on-Tees, Redcar, Billingham, Hartlepool and the Tees Valley. There is no page for Stockton-on-Tees, Redcar, Billingham or Hartlepool. Those towns appear only as names in lists on `/`, `/services`, `/web-design-teesside`, `/about`, `/contact` and `/resources` (and once, Billingham, in a chat example on `/ai-automation-middlesbrough`), in JSON-LD `areaServed`, and in the footer. Google ranks pages, not footers. Hard rule 2 applies: any such page needs real, local, unique content, so the owner has to supply what is true about each place (owner question 3).

**C5. Title and description length failures.** 22 of them, listed by `seo:check`. Easy to fix, but 18 of 21 snippets are being truncated or rewritten by Google today.

**I1. No location signal in the body of six pages.** Zero area names in the body copy of `/landing-page-design`, `/crm-booking-automation`, `/website-care`, `/how-it-works`, `/pricing` and `/med-spa-growth-system`. `/work` says only "Teesside" once. The titles of `/website-care`, `/work`, `/how-it-works`, `/pricing` and `/med-spa-growth-system` carry no place name either, and the `/med-spa-growth-system` H1 has none. `/local-seo-middlesbrough` mentions Middlesbrough once in its body and not in its H1.

**I2. Weak internal linking from the sales pages.** Each of the five sales pages links in its body to only two pages, `/free-website-audit` and `/pricing`. `serviceSalesPage()` never calls the `related()` helper that `/google-business-profile`, `/website-care` and `/web-design-teesside` use, so there are no links between web design, local SEO, GBP, landing pages and automation, which is where topical relevance is passed between money pages. Eight pages in all have fewer than three body links out: the five sales pages, `/contact`, `/privacy` and `/cookie-policy`.

**I3. Low contextual inbound links.** Nine of 21 pages have fewer than three other pages linking to them from body copy (header and footer excluded): `/` (1, from the 404), `/about` (1), `/web-design-teesside` (1), `/resources` (1), `/terms` (1), `/cookie-policy` (1), `/services` (2), `/how-it-works` (2), `/google-business-profile` (2). `/web-design-teesside` is the area hub and is in neither the nav nor the footer; the homepage is its only inbound link.

**I4. Thin or template-heavy content.** The sales pages are 542-629 words, much of it shared structure (old way, offer stack, bonuses, "Total Real-World Value", guarantee). `/work` has 417 words and one real project; `/contact` has 415. `/resources` packs four guides into one 938-word page, so none can rank on its own. `/web-design-teesside` (697 words) and `/google-business-profile` (707) are fuller and carry FAQs.

**I5. Duplicate and near-duplicate copy.**
- The plan/booking form block is repeated: `/contact` and `/free-website-audit` each share about 50% of their 6-word phrases with `/pricing` and with each other, and `/` shares about 24% with each. That is one shared form section, a structural repeat rather than copying, but it means `/contact` is largely a form.
- The closing H2 "Know what your website needs before you spend money on it." appears on 8 pages (`/`, `/web-design-teesside`, `/google-business-profile`, `/website-care`, `/how-it-works`, `/about`, `/pricing`) and is the H1 of `/free-website-audit`.
- The five sales pages share one template, so phrases such as "Total Real-World Value", "Fixed price. Zero hidden fees." and "…or we work for free" recur. Individually the text differs; as a set the pages read as one page repeated.

**I6. Redirects.** `vercel.json` and `src/pages.js` agree on every redirect once Vercel's `cleanUrls` behaviour is taken into account, and the checker confirms it. Three things to know:
- `src/pages.js` lists `.html` variants (for example `/ai-receptionist-for-med-spas.html`) that `vercel.json` does not list. With `cleanUrls: true` Vercel first redirects `/x.html` to `/x` and then applies the real redirect, so the three old med-spa `.html` URLs take two hops where the dev server takes one.
- `src/pages.js` lists `/marketing-services` exactly, and `vercel.json` has only `/marketing-services/:path*`. In Vercel's path matching the `*` makes the trailing part optional, so the bare path should be covered. That has not been tested on a live deployment.
- The list is maintained in two places, so it can drift again. `seo:check` now guards it.

**I7. Structured data gaps.**
- The five sales pages emit `Service` with `provider: {"@id": ".../#organization"}` but do not include the `ProfessionalService` node that defines that `@id`, so the reference dangles on those pages. *Fixed 13 Oct 2026: `src/layout.js` now emits the organisation graph on every page, and `scripts/seo-check.mjs` fails any page with an unresolved `@id`.*
- No page has `sameAs`, `address` or review markup. That is deliberate and correct under rule 1, but the owner should know a service-area Google Business Profile is the missing off-site half (owner question 4).
- `FAQPage` markup is on 9 pages. Google limits FAQ rich results to a few site types, so it adds no visible benefit. It is harmless.

**I8. Unlabelled invented rating on the homepage and services visuals.** The decorative card on `/` and `/services` shows "Ashfield Joinery 4.9 (86 reviews)" with a star row (`src/visuals.js:172`). The graphic is `aria-hidden` and a code comment calls it illustrative, but nothing visible says so. It is not in JSON-LD, so no structured-data rule is broken, but it is an invented review count on the homepage.

## 5. Questions for the owner

`docs/seo/owner-todo.md` does not exist yet, though hard rule 1 expects it. No TODO lines were added in this part.

1. Are any of the five "client" case studies real? If so, which, and is there written permission and evidence (a Search Console export, call logs, an invoice, a testimonial)? If none are real, they should be removed or clearly relabelled.
2. Will the business honour the "or we work for free" guarantees in writing? If yes, the terms need to say what triggers them. If not, they should go.
