# 30-60-90 day plan

Starts on the handover date, 13 October 2026. Day 30 is about 12 November, day 60 about 12 December and day 90 about 11 January 2027.

Expect slow progress. A new page usually takes 4-12 weeks to collect impressions in Search Console, and local rankings move over months, not weeks. Nothing here guarantees a ranking. The aim is to give Google steady, honest reasons to trust the site.

Rules that apply to all of it (from `.cursor/rules/seo.mdc`): no invented reviews, results, clients or figures; no copy-and-paste town pages; plain UK English; and run `npm test` and `npm run build` after every site change.

## Days 1-30: get indexed and set up the basics

### Do

- **Search Console.** Follow `launch-checklist.md` section 2: confirm the property, submit the sitemap, and request indexing for the 10 priority URLs. Add the site to Bing Webmaster Tools (section 3).
- **Rich Results Test.** Test the URLs in `launch-checklist.md` section 4 and note any errors.
- **Google Business Profile.** If it is not live yet, set it up as a service area business with no public address (the steps are in `/resources/google-business-profile-service-area-business`). Use exactly the same business name, phone number and service areas as the website. Then send the profile URL so it can go into `SITE.sameAs`.
- **Reviews: set up the habit.** Get the review link from the Business Profile (**Ask for reviews**). After every finished job, send it to the customer with a short, personal message. Ask every customer, not only the happy ones; Google's policies forbid selective asking and any reward for reviews. Keep a simple list of who was asked and when.
- **Citations: the foundations.** Using the copy in `docs/off-page-seo/listing-kit.md`, set up the foundation listings in `docs/off-page-seo/tracker.csv`: Bing Places (F02, which can import from Google Business Profile), Apple Business (F03), the LinkedIn company page (S01) and the Facebook business page (S02). Use identical details everywhere and update each row's status in the tracker. Yell (D02) is skipped by your decision of 9 October, because it would publish a street address.
- **Articles: publish 2.** Pick from the phase 2 list in `keyword-map.md` section 5. Good first choices, because they support money pages: "How much does website maintenance cost?" (supports `/website-care`) and "AI receptionist vs answering service" (supports `/ai-receptionist-middlesbrough`). Each needs a unique title and description, one H1, at least 3 links in and out, and an entry in the keyword map.
- **Owner answers.** Work through the top of the list in `owner-summary.md`, especially the facts on the area pages and the illustrative visuals on the homepage and `/services`.

## Days 31-60: build trust and content

### Do

- **Search Console, every two weeks.** Pages report: is anything new not indexed? Performance: which queries have started showing? Write new query ideas in the notes column of `rank-tracking.csv`.
- **Articles: publish 2-4 more** (4-6 so far). Choose topics from real questions customers ask on calls and in the chat, and from Search Console queries the site shows for but has no page about. Link each one to its money page, and link the money page back.
- **Real case studies: start the first one.** Choose one finished project where the client agrees in writing to be named. Collect before-and-after screenshots, what was done, a timeline, and any figures the client can back up (enquiries, calls, bookings). Publish it on `/work` only with that evidence. It needs a card style without the "Illustrative example" label (see `claims-review.md`). One real case study is worth more than any number of examples.
- **Citations: the directories in the tracker.** Next in `tracker.csv`: FreeIndex (D01; read the address note in `listing-kit.md` first), the Teesside University alumni directory (D08, if eligible), Findit (D06), the agency directories Clutch, Sortlist and TechBehemoths (D03-D05), and the local council and community directories (N12, N13). Check every listing shows exactly the same name, phone and service areas.
- **Local links.** Work through `docs/off-page-seo/outreach-drafts.md`: the Tees Business pitch, Tees Valley Business Club, NetWORK NotWORK, Entrepreneurs' Forum and the council business teams (tracker rows N and P). Add suppliers and partners you already work with, and any client sites where a "Website by Veltra Media" footer credit is welcome. Aim for a few genuine, relevant links, not many weak ones. Never buy links.
- **Business Profile.** Post an update every one to two weeks (a finished project, a tip, a new article) and add real photos of your work.

## Days 61-90: improve what is close

### Do

- **Re-optimise pages at positions 8-20.** These pages are close to page one, so improving them pays off faster than writing new ones. For each one:
  1. In Performance, filter **Page** = that URL and open the **Queries** tab. Note the queries with the most impressions.
  2. Check that the main query appears naturally in the title, H1, opening paragraph and at least one H2. Do not stuff it in.
  3. Answer what those searchers want and the page does not yet cover: prices, timescales, the towns they name, common questions. Add a real local detail or example if you have one.
  4. Add 2-3 links to the page from related pages and articles, using descriptive anchor text.
  5. Rewrite the meta description so it matches the query and gives a reason to click. A page with many impressions but few clicks needs this most.
  6. Change only one or two pages at a time, and note the date in `rank-tracking.csv`, so you can see what worked. Allow 3-4 weeks before judging.
- **Articles: publish 2-4 more** (6-10 so far).
- **Case studies: publish another** if a second client agrees. Link each one from the matching service page and town page.
- **Reviews.** Keep asking after every job. Answer every review, good or bad, politely and without personal details.
- **Phase 2 pages.** Check `keyword-map.md` section 5 against Search Console. Build a phase 2 page (for example `/missed-call-text-back` or a trade-specific page) only if the queries show demand and you have real, specific content for it.
- **Citations.** Fix any inconsistent listings. Add niche directories for your sectors (trades, med spa) only where they are genuine and relevant.

## Every month, in short

1. Fill in `rank-tracking.csv` (about 30 minutes; see `README.md`).
2. Check the Pages report for anything new that is not indexed.
3. Publish 2-4 articles.
4. Ask every customer for a review.
5. Add or fix a few citations and look for one or two genuine local links.
6. Improve one or two pages that sit at positions 8-20.
7. After any site change: `npm test`, `npm run build`, and after deploy `npm run seo:crawl -- https://www.veltramedia.com --live`.
