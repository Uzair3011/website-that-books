# Veltra Media: Cursor prompt pack for Tees Valley SEO

Written 12 Oct 2026. Built from a read of the live site and this repo (static Node generator, Vercel, 21 pages, `src/pages.js` registry, `scripts/build.mjs` sitemap).

## What I found before writing these

1. **The site has too few pages to rank in six places.** There are pages for Middlesbrough and "Teesside", but none for Stockton-on-Tees, Redcar, Billingham or Hartlepool. The six areas only appear in schema and footer text. Google ranks pages, not footers.
2. **The money-page H1s are sales-letter headlines, not keywords.** Example: `/web-design-middlesbrough` has the H1 "How Teesside Service Businesses Are Getting 3x More Local Leads Without Spending a Single Penny More on Ads." The page you want ranking for "web design Middlesbrough" does not say that phrase in its H1.
3. **Indexing is unconfirmed.** Your own `docs/off-page-seo/README.md` says nobody has confirmed which Google account holds Search Console. A `site:veltramedia.com` search and a "Veltra Media" search both returned none of your pages in my tool. That is only a hint (my search tool is not Google), but check it first. If the pages are not indexed, nothing below will show until they are.
4. **Competition.** For "web design Middlesbrough" the results are Outrank, Passionates, Thrive, JF Web, Yarm Webcraft and directories (TechBehemoths, Sortlist, BizHub365). For "AI automation agency Teesside" my search returned only news and Tees Valley Combined Authority pages, no agencies. That is a real gap. One search, so treat it as indicative.
5. **"AI agents" on its own is not winnable.** Target "AI agents for small businesses in Teesside", "AI receptionist Middlesbrough" and similar.
6. **Some on-site claims look unverifiable** (for example "312% more calls", "2 to 18 leads a month", "£3,800 of value"). Your repo notes say the mockups are illustrative. Real client proof helps rankings and conversion, and unproven results can breach UK advertising rules. The prompts below tell Cursor to flag these, not to invent anything.
