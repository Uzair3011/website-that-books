Written 12 Oct 2026 on branch `seo/tees-valley`, from `.cursor/rules/seo.mdc` and `audit-2026-10.md`. Updated the same day with the owner's decisions (section 6). On 23 Oct 2026 the H1s for `/`, `/services`, `/work` and `/about` were rewritten as promises after a design review; each keeps its primary keyword. No site code was changed for this document. It sets which page targets which search phrase, so new and rewritten pages do not compete with each other.

**Read this first.**

- Every keyword comes from the owner's seed list. Close variants and long-tail phrases that are not in the seed list are marked **(added)**. The owner approved all of them on 12 Oct 2026. The mark only shows where a phrase came from.
- **Search volume for every keyword here is unknown. Verify it in Search Console / Keyword Planner** before you spend time on a page because of its keyword. Nothing in this document is based on live Google data.
- These broad terms are **never a primary target**: "web design", "SEO", "AI", "ai agents", "marketing agency". They can appear naturally in copy.
- Titles are 60 characters or fewer (checked with a script; `£` counts as one). A few drop the "| Veltra Media" suffix to fit. Google shows the site name separately in results, so this is acceptable. Wherever the brand appears it is "Veltra Media", never "Veltra" alone.
- "Tradesmen" goes in titles and H1s because it is the phrase people search. Body copy says "trades" or "tradespeople".
- The proposed H1s replace the current sales headlines on five pages. They also drop the unsupported "3x more leads" style claims (audit C1 and C2). Body copy for each page still has to follow hard rules 1, 2 and 5.

If a literal 301 is required, use `"statusCode": 301`. In that case, check that `seo:check` still compares the rule correctly, and change it if not.
7. After deploy, ask Google to index the new URL in Search Console. Also change any external listing or Google Business Profile link that points at the old URL.

## 4. Why these primaries

- **`/`**: Searches for "Veltra" alone return Veltra Corp (Tokyo), so the homepage owns the brand. Its title and H1 name the services and Teesside, so it is not brand-only, but they stay clear of the exact phrases the web design pages own.
- **`/services`**: It is a hub with links to every service, so it gets a broad, non-competing phrase rather than any one service's keyword.
- **`/web-design-middlesbrough`**: The URL, the business's base and the core service all match the highest-intent seed, "web design Middlesbrough".
- **`/web-design-teesside`**: It is the only page with regional content, and its H1 already contains "web design Teesside", so it keeps that phrase and takes the Tees Valley variants.
- **`/local-seo-middlesbrough`**: The URL already says "local SEO Middlesbrough". As the only SEO page in this phase, it also takes the Teesside SEO phrases.
- **`/google-business-profile`**: It is a separate, priced £99 service, and "Google Business Profile optimisation Middlesbrough" is the seed that describes what that service delivers.
- **`/landing-page-design`**: The seed "landing page design Middlesbrough" matches the page, and the move to Middlesbrough gives it the same place pattern as the other service pages.
- **`/ai-receptionist-middlesbrough`**: The page sells an AI receptionist and chat, and the new URL, title and H1 all say so.
- **`/crm-booking-automation`**: "booking and CRM automation for small business" is the only seed that describes this service, and nobody searches for it with a town name attached.
- **`/website-care`**: "website maintenance" is the word owners more commonly search for than the brand-ish "website care", which stays as a secondary.
- **`/work`**: "web design portfolio Teesside" describes what a buyer looking at past work wants.
- **`/how-it-works`**: "website design process" matches what the page explains.
- **`/about`**: It is a brand page, and it should answer "about Veltra Media" without taking a service keyword.
- **`/free-website-audit`**: "free website audit" is the page's actual offer and its main lead source.
- **`/contact`**: It is a brand and navigation page, with no service keyword to protect.
- **`/resources`**: It becomes the hub for the articles, so it gets a broad guide phrase that none of them use.
- **`/pricing`**: "website cost Middlesbrough" is commercial and local, and the pricing page is where every published price answers it.
- **`/med-spa-growth-system`**: The page took over the old `/med-spa-website-design` URL and sells mostly to med spas, so "med spa website design" matches both its history and its buyer.
- **`/privacy`, `/cookie-policy`, `/terms`**: Legal pages are not search targets.
- **`/web-design-stockton-on-tees`**: Stockton-on-Tees is the largest named town after Middlesbrough. Billingham is in the same borough, so one page with a real Billingham section beats two thin ones.
- **`/web-design-redcar`**: "web design Redcar" is the seed for this town, and the page also takes "SEO Redcar" so the town does not need a second, thinner page.
- **`/web-design-hartlepool`**: "web design Hartlepool" is the seed, and the page also takes "SEO Hartlepool" for the same reason as Redcar.
- **`/areas`**: It is a list of links, so it gets a navigation phrase that cannot compete with any service page.
- **`/ai-agents-teesside`**: "AI agents Teesside" matches the URL and adds a place to the unwinnable head term "ai agents". As the overview page, it also takes the national "AI agents for small business UK".
