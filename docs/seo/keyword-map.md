# Keyword map, October 2026

Written 12 Oct 2026 on branch `seo/tees-valley`, from `.cursor/rules/seo.mdc` and `audit-2026-10.md`. No site code was changed for this document. It sets which page targets which search phrase, so new and rewritten pages do not compete with each other.

**Read this first.**

- Every keyword comes from the owner's seed list. Close variants and long-tail phrases that are not in the seed list are marked **(added)**. Where a page has no matching seed (about, contact, work and so on), its phrase is marked **(added)** too.
- **Search volume for every keyword here is unknown. Verify it in Search Console / Keyword Planner** before you spend time on a page because of its keyword. Nothing in this document is based on live Google data.
- These broad terms are **never a primary target**: "web design", "SEO", "AI", "ai agents", "marketing agency". They can appear naturally in copy.
- Titles are 60 characters or fewer (checked with a script; `£` counts as one). A few drop the "| Veltra Media" suffix to fit. Google shows the site name separately in results, so this is acceptable. Wherever the brand appears it is "Veltra Media", never "Veltra" alone.
- The proposed H1s replace the current sales headlines on five pages. They also drop the unsupported "3x more leads" style claims (audit C1 and C2). Body copy for each page still has to follow hard rules 1, 2 and 5.

## 3. Why these primaries

- **`/`**: Searches for "Veltra" alone return Veltra Corp (Tokyo), so the homepage needs to own the brand. Giving it "web design Middlesbrough" as well would compete with the page built for that phrase.
- **`/services`**: It is a hub with links to every service, so it gets a broad, non-competing phrase rather than any one service's keyword.
- **`/web-design-middlesbrough`**: The URL, the business's base and the core service all match the highest-intent seed, "web design Middlesbrough".
- **`/web-design-teesside`**: It is the only page with regional content, and its H1 already contains "web design Teesside", so it keeps that phrase and takes the Tees Valley variants.
- **`/local-seo-middlesbrough`**: The URL already says "local SEO Middlesbrough", and the phrase is more specific about the service than "SEO Middlesbrough", which it takes as a secondary.
- **`/google-business-profile`**: It is a separate, priced £99 service, and "Google Business Profile optimisation Middlesbrough" is the seed that describes what that service delivers.
- **`/landing-page-design`**: The seed "landing page design Middlesbrough" matches the page, and the move to Middlesbrough gives it the same place pattern as the other service pages.
- **`/ai-automation-middlesbrough`**: The page sells an AI receptionist and chat, and its current title already says "AI Receptionist", so "AI receptionist Middlesbrough" is the phrase its content can satisfy.
- **`/crm-booking-automation`**: "booking and CRM automation for small business" is the only seed that describes this service, and nobody searches for it with a town name attached.
- **`/website-care`**: "website maintenance" is the word owners more commonly search for than the brand-ish "website care", which stays as a secondary.
- **`/work`**: No seed fits, and "web design portfolio Teesside" describes what a buyer looking at past work wants.
- **`/how-it-works`**: No seed fits, and "website design process" matches what the page explains.
- **`/about`**: It is a brand page, and it should answer "about Veltra Media" without taking a service keyword.
- **`/free-website-audit`**: No seed fits, and "free website audit" is the page's actual offer and its main lead source.
- **`/contact`**: It is a brand and navigation page, with no service keyword to protect.
- **`/resources`**: It becomes the hub for the articles, so it gets a broad guide phrase that none of them use.
- **`/pricing`**: "website cost Middlesbrough" is commercial and local, and the pricing page is where every published price answers it.
- **`/med-spa-growth-system`**: The page took over the old `/med-spa-website-design` URL and sells mostly to med spas, so "med spa website design" matches both its history and its buyer.
- **`/privacy`, `/cookie-policy`, `/terms`**: Legal pages are not search targets.
- **`/web-design-stockton-on-tees`**: Stockton-on-Tees is the largest named town after Middlesbrough, and its web design seed is the phrase a local buyer types.
- **`/web-design-redcar`**: "web design Redcar" is the seed for this town, and the page also takes "SEO Redcar" so the town does not need a second, thinner page.
- **`/web-design-billingham`**: "web design Billingham" is the seed, as long as there are enough real local facts to fill a page (decision 4).
- **`/web-design-hartlepool`**: "web design Hartlepool" is the seed, and the page also takes "SEO Hartlepool" for the same reason as Redcar.
- **`/areas`**: It is a navigation hub, so it gets a phrase that cannot compete with `/web-design-teesside` or `/local-seo-teesside`.
- **`/local-seo-teesside`**: "local SEO Teesside" matches the URL and the Middlesbrough page's naming pattern, and it gathers "SEO company Teesside" and "SEO agency Tees Valley" in one place.
- **`/ai-agents-teesside`**: "AI agents Teesside" matches the URL and adds a place to the unwinnable head term "ai agents".
- **`/web-design-for-trades-teesside`**: "website design for tradesmen Teesside" is the seed for the second-strongest niche in `docs/positioning.md`.
- **`/resources/how-much-does-a-website-cost`**: It answers the seed question nationally and sends local buyers on to `/pricing`.
- **`/resources/do-i-need-a-website-if-i-have-facebook`**: It is the exact seed question, which a page-one answer can satisfy.
- **`/resources/local-seo-checklist`**: It is a short, exact seed that suits a practical article and links back to both local SEO pages.
- **`/resources/google-business-profile-service-area-business`**: It covers two seeds with the same intent, since a business without a shop is a service area business, and combining them avoids two thin articles.
- **`/resources/what-is-an-ai-receptionist`**: It is the exact seed question and the first step for a buyer who is new to the idea.
- **`/resources/ai-agents-for-small-business`**: It takes the national, research-stage AI agents seed, so `/ai-agents-teesside` can stay local and commercial.

## 4. Phase 2: pages to build later, ranked

Ranked on closeness to a paid service, how well the seed list supports the page, and the risk of competing with an existing page. For each, check demand in Search Console / Keyword Planner first. Volume is unknown.

1. **`/missed-call-text-back`**: "missed call text back UK" is a seed and a specific feature that buyers search for. When this page goes live, move the phrase from `/ai-automation-middlesbrough` to it.

5. **Town-specific SEO pages** (for example `/local-seo-stockton-on-tees`): only if Search Console shows a town page earning impressions for its "SEO + town" phrase without ranking for it. Until then the town pages own those phrases.
