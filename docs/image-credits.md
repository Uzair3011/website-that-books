# Image credits

The concept project images (`assets/work/concept-*.webp`) are mock-ups of
fictional businesses' websites, drawn in HTML by Veltra Media and rendered with
`node scripts/concept-mockups/render.mjs`. They contain no third-party photos;
the typefaces (Archivo, Barlow, Fraunces, Inter) are Google Fonts under the SIL
Open Font License.

When a real client project replaces a concept, use a screenshot of the client's
site (or a photo the client supplies) and record its source here.

`assets/work/tees-transporter-bridge.webp` is a photo of the Tees Transporter
Bridge, Middlesbrough, supplied by the site owner and resized to 1600×1047
(`tees-transporter-bridge-800.webp` is the same photo at 800 px for phones). The
photographer and licence were not recorded when it was added; add them here.

`assets/work/project-estate-resolve.webp` is a screenshot of the homepage of
https://www.estateresolve.co.uk/ (a Veltra build), supplied by the site owner on
2026-10-21. The side margins were trimmed and the top extended in the site's own
navy so it fills a 1280×900 canvas.

`assets/work/project-med-spa.webp` is a screenshot of the Lumière Aesthetics
homepage design for the med-spa growth system, supplied by the site owner on
2026-10-21. It was cropped to remove the bottom edge and centred on a 1280×900
canvas in the screenshot's own background colour. It replaced the Burst photo
"Woman at spa getting facial".

`assets/work/hero-med-spa.webp` is the same Lumière Aesthetics screenshot with its
side margins trimmed, used at the top of /med-spa-growth-system. It replaced the
illustration `assets/work/example-clinic.svg`, which was deleted.
